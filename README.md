# Logivra Compliance Hub

Build a complete, high converting, responsive landing page for Logivra using a clean modern design and Tailwind CSS. Use real copy only and do not include any placeholder text like [insert headline here].

## THE PRODUCT

Logivra is an automated environmental and EHS compliance platform. It uses intelligent document processing to read permits, regulations, and reports, turning them into specific, trackable compliance obligations. It is built for EHS managers at manufacturing plants to remove the risk of human error in regulatory tracking and prevent expensive environmental violations.

## THE CUSTOMER

EHS Managers at mid-sized manufacturing plants with 100 to 500 employees. They currently use fragmented spreadsheets and manually read through hundreds of pages of federal and state permits. They are worried about missing a reporting deadline, failing an audit, or facing personal liability for a violation they simply didn't see coming in a wall of text.

## PAGE STRUCTURE

1. Hero: The headline is 'Turn complex permits into actionable compliance tasks'. The subheadline is 'Logivra automatically extracts deadlines and obligations from your regulatory documents so you never miss a reporting date or face a fine'. The primary button text is 'Request a platform demo'. The secondary link text is 'See how it works'. The hero visual should be a clean interface showing a document on the left and a clear, dated task list on the right.

2. Problem: 'My spreadsheets are a mess and I am terrified of missing one sentence in a 50-page permit.' 'I spend more time reading regulations than I do improving safety on the floor.' 'An overlooked deadline could cost this facility tens of thousands of dollars in fines.'

3. How it works:

- Upload your permits: Drag and drop your facility permits and regulatory documents into the secure portal.

- Automated extraction: The system reads every line to identify specific obligations, deadlines, and reporting requirements.

- Manage by exception: Receive alerts for upcoming deadlines and store evidence of completion in one central audit trail.

4. Features:

- Intelligent permit parsing: Automatically convert dense PDF permits into a structured list of tasks and dates.

- Proactive alert system: Get email and dashboard notifications long before a compliance deadline arrives.

- Audit-ready evidence vault: Upload and link inspection reports or logs directly to the specific regulatory requirement.

- Multi-site dashboard: View the compliance health of every facility in your network from a single screen.

5. Social proof:

- 'This has completely changed how we handle our annual environmental reporting.' - EHS Manager, Industrial Manufacturing

- 'I no longer worry about a surprise audit because all our evidence is organized and linked to the right permits.' - Environmental Coordinator, Chemicals Plant

- 'We moved away from a dozen disconnected spreadsheets to one clear source of truth.' - Director of Safety, Automotive Components

6. Pricing:

- Single Facility Plan: Starting at $10,000 per year. Includes full permit parsing, deadline tracking, and evidence storage for one physical site.

- Complex Operations Plan: Up to $25,000 per year. Designed for facilities in highly regulated zones with high-frequency reporting requirements and multiple environmental permits.

7. FAQ:

- How accurate is the document parsing? The system identifies obligations based on your specific permits and highlights the exact text it used so you can verify the task immediately.

- Can this replace our current EHS software? Logivra is designed to focus specifically on the permit-to-task workflow which most general EHS software lacks.

- Does it handle state-specific regulations? Yes, the platform processes permits issued at the local, state, and federal levels.

- How long does implementation take? Most facilities are up and running as soon as their current permits are uploaded and processed.

- Is our data secure? We use enterprise-grade encryption for all uploaded documents and evidence logs to ensure facility privacy.

8. Final call to action: 'Stop managing compliance in spreadsheets.' Include an email capture form with the button text 'Get Started'.

9. Simple footer: Logivra. Dedicated to manufacturing compliance. Links for Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## DESIGN DIRECTION

Modern, industrial, and trustworthy. Use a professional color palette of deep navy blue, slate gray, and a highlight color of forest green to signify environmental compliance. Typography should be a clean sans-serif like Inter or Helvetica. Use plenty of white space and clear borders. The imagery should be high-quality and professional, focusing on clean factory environments and software interfaces. Ensure the layout is fully responsive and looks sharp on mobile devices.

## COPY RULES

Use plain language. Avoid marketing buzzwords like 'synergy' or 'revolutionary'. Keep sentences short and punchy. Focus on the benefits of peace of mind and time saved. Do not invent fake statistics about how many millions of dollars users have saved.
